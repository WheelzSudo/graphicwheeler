I’m graphicwheeler — a lifelong Minnesotan, gamer, Trekkie, husband, and proud dad to two daughters. Based in Sartell, I’m the Head Graphic Designer at D.J.V. Label & Packaging, creating label and packaging artwork for flexographic and thermal printing.

I earned my associate degree in E-commerce and Web Marketing in 2004. Star Trek sparked my love of computers; creating things kept me hooked. Off the clock, it’s gaming as Wheelz_TTV, TV and movies tracked on Trakt, and family time. Streaming is on hiatus for now.

Proud girl dad. Live long and prosper.
